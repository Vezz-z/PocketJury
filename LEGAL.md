# PocketJury AI Legal Architecture & Sourcing

This document outlines the operational architecture of the PocketJury AI Legal Assistant, specifying how legal information is generated, the prompt engineering strategies employed, and the exact sources of the legal references provided to users.

---

## 1. Core Architecture: RAG vs Fine-Tuning

**PocketJury does NOT rely on a fine-tuned LLM.** Instead, it utilizes a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipeline.

### Why RAG?
Large Language Models (LLMs) are notorious for "hallucinating" or fabricating legal precedents and section numbers. In the legal domain, absolute accuracy is critical. By using a RAG pipeline, the LLM is strictly forbidden from "guessing" the law from its baseline training data. 

Instead, when a user asks a question:
1. The system converts the user's query into a vector embedding (`intfloat/multilingual-e5-large`).
2. It performs a semantic search against an isolated database of **verified statutes**.
3. The exact, raw legal text from these statutes is injected directly into the LLM's prompt.
4. The LLM acts solely as a reasoning and simplification engine, translating the provided legal text into accessible language for the user.

---

## 2. Prompt Engineering & Safeguards

The generation is strictly controlled via Prompt Engineering (defined in `services/ai/app/core/prompt_templates.py`). The LLM is given a severe SYSTEM PROMPT that establishes its mechanical boundaries:

*   **Identified Constraint:** "You are NOT a lawyer and NOT providing legal advice... You provide LEGAL INFORMATION and EDUCATIONAL GUIDANCE only."
*   **Citation Mandate:** "ALWAYS cite specific sections, acts, or case law when available from the retrieved context."
*   **Fabrication Ban:** "NEVER fabricate section numbers, case names, or legal provisions. If the retrieved context doesn't contain sufficient information... clearly state what you can and cannot answer."
*   **Mandatory Mapping:** Every mention of an outdated Indian Penal Code (IPC) section is systematically forced via prompt injection to reference the new Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023 equivalent.
*   **Safety Thresholds:** The prompt structure automatically intercepts sensitive queries (Domestic Violence, Child Abuse) and forces the LLM to output official National Helplines (e.g., 181, 1098, 1930).

---

## 3. Verified Legal Sources and Datasets

All legal references generated by PocketJury originate exclusively from the system's PostgreSQL Vector Database (`document_embeddings` table). 

This database is seeded using the `seed_legal_docs.py` script. **The exclusive source for all legal text and statutes is the Legislative Department, Government of India.**

### Official Website Sourcing
*   **Primary Source:** [https://legislative.gov.in/](https://legislative.gov.in/) (Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice)

### Conclusion for the Jury
The underlying LLM is an unmodified foundation model (e.g., Claude 3.5 Sonnet / LLaMA 3.1). **It is not legally trained.** The legal integrity of PocketJury is maintained by overriding the LLM's baseline knowledge with explicit, exact text scraped directly from the Government of India's legislative portal entirely through the RAG context window.
